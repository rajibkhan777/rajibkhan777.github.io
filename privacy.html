Privacy Policy

Healthguide360 Automation · Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how "Healthguide360 Automation" (the "App") handles information. The App is a private automation tool operated by the owner of the Healthguide360-বাংলা channels for their own use only. It is not available to the public.

1. Who uses the App

Only the channel owner signs in to the App, using their own Google, YouTube and Meta accounts. Viewers of our videos never sign in to or interact with the App.

2. What data the App accesses

  • Google Drive: creates and organises files (research notes, scripts, prompts, video files and thumbnails) inside the owner's own Drive folders.
  • Google Sheets: reads and updates the owner's production-tracking spreadsheet.
  • YouTube: uploads and schedules videos on the owner's own channel and reads that channel's own analytics (views, watch time).
  • Meta (Facebook Page, Instagram): publishes content to the owner's own Page and account and reads their own insights, if connected.

3. How the data is used

Data is used only to prepare, store, schedule and measure the owner's own videos. The App does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, does not use it for advertising, and does not collect personal data from viewers.

4. Where data is stored

Files stay in the owner's own Google Drive and Google Sheets. Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer in a self-hosted n8n installation. No separate server or database stores this information.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Retention and deletion

Working files are moved to Google Drive Trash about 7 days after a video is published. The owner can revoke the App's access at any time from Google Account permissions or Meta's app settings, which stops all access.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the owner's GitHub profile: github.com/rajibkhan777.
