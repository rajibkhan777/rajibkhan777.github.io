Healthguide360-বাংলা creates short, science-based Bangla videos about food, nutrition and the human body, published on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

Healthguide360 Automation

"Healthguide360 Automation" is a private tool used only by the channel owner to prepare and organise these videos (research notes, scripts, files in the owner's own Google Drive, and scheduling uploads to the owner's own channels). It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from viewers.

Important: our videos are for general education only and are not medical advice. Please consult a qualified doctor for personal health decisions.
